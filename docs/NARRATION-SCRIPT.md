# Tivora ERP films: explanatory narration script (v2, draft for approval)

Why this exists: v1 only read the on-screen captions aloud. This version **explains** each screen: what it is, why a business owner cares, and how the pieces connect. The on-screen captions stay as they are (short labels); the voice adds the meaning.

Rules for every line: only what the app does today (nothing added beyond the real screens), no "AI" wording, no "IRD certified", no prices or promised results, HiTech is named as the maker, Jewelry is "available now", General trading and Paint are "next". Letters are spelled the way they are spoken (B S, A D, V A T, T D S, I R D).

Format: `t` = start second, `fit` = seconds the line must fit in, then the line. About 2.4 words a second.
